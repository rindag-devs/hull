# WASIp1 WITX provenance

The WITX sources in this directory are derived from the canonical WASI
Preview1 definitions distributed with the Wasmtime project's `wasmtime-wasi`
crate. They are vendored here and may be modified as needed for Hull's WASIp1
bindings.
